    The dashed line is a straight line. Walls are not on the plan: follow corridors and escape-route signs.

    Set up the building plan

    Draw walls: click where a wall starts, then where it ends. Walls are always drawn horizontal or vertical. Select wall: click a wall, then delete it. Add door: click on a wall to open a door there (green); click a green door again to remove it. Set the plan width in metres first. Walls are only used to work out walking routes and are not shown to employees.

    PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG up to 10 MB. Export a PDF to an image first. The width in metres is used to estimate distances.

    What is happening?

    Emergency call: the 5 Ws

    1. Where is the fire? Place, street, number, floor
    2. What happened? Type of fire
    3. How many people are affected?
    4. What injuries are there?
    5. Wait for questions. Hang up only when the dispatcher says so.

    Trapped?

    • Close the door and seal gaps with wet cloths
    • Go to a window, shout and wave
    • Call 112, give exact location and floor
    • Never jump from a great height
    • Stay low if smoke is in the room

    This page gives general guidance from approved documents and does not replace the fire service or medical care. Distances on the plan are estimates. When in doubt: leave the danger area and call 112.
